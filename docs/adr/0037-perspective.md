# ADR-37: Effect ▸ Perspective

- Status: accepted (Phase 4, 2026-11-11; decided without the owner where marked, owner questions listed at the end)
- Context:
  - DeluxePaint IV AGA turns the custom brush in 3D and paints it in perspective: Effect ▸ Perspective ▸ Do (keypad Enter), FillScreen (keypad `-`), Reset (keypad `0`), Center (keypad `.`) and Settings.. (also a right click on Grid while the mode is on). In the mode the brush is a wire frame of four cells, the keypad turns it about X, Y and Z (Shift: by the Angle Step), the mouse slides it on its plane, `;` `'` move it along its fixed axis (Shift: `:` `"`, by a larger step; the tutorial once calls them `@` and `:`, p.134–135), `<` `>` move the observer, Ctrl fixes Y, `\` switches the title bar between angles and position; a click paints it; clicking a tool leaves the mode, but the tutorial clicks CLR in the mode and goes on turning, and changes the handle with Alt-x there (tutorial AGA p.126–145, CLR p.131, handle p.142, menu p.278, requester p.279–281, title bar p.286, keys p.295).
  - The Perspective requester: Grid X / Y / Z (brush size, Z = Y), From Brush, Angle Step (90), Type Screen (Euler) / Brush, Display Angle / Pos, Cancel, Ok (p.279–281). Fill Type also has a Perspective fill (p.211).
  - egui 0.37 cannot tell the keypad from the main keys: egui-winit maps `Numpad0`–`Numpad9` to `Num0`–`Num9`, keypad Enter to Enter, keypad `-` / `.` to Minus / Period, and drops the key location. Main-row `1`–`6` are animation keys (ADR-28). egui has no key for `>`, `"` or `@`: for those it reports the key's place (Shift-. on a US keyboard comes as Period with Shift, then the text `>`).
  - Rule: no pixel logic outside `dpx-core`/`dpx-raster`; one Command engine for GUI, CLI and MCP.
- Decision:
  - **Core (`dpx-core::perspective`).** Page pixels are x right, y down, z into the screen; the screen is the plane z = 0, the observer stands `distance` in front of the centre. Orientation is a 3×3 matrix (columns: the brush's axes). Type Screen keeps Euler angles, M = Rx·Ry·Rz; Type Brush turns about the brush's own axes and reads the angles back out of the matrix. Sines come from a table of whole degrees (exact at 0/30/45/60/90), so every result is deterministic. Projection is inverse ray casting: each page pixel's centre ray meets the brush plane, the hit is mapped to the nearest brush pixel; rays nearer than 1 pixel of depth or beyond 64 × distance (planes only) paint nothing. Results are trimmed brush images placed at their top-left corner. `fill_plane` tiles a whole plane by the grid; `wireframe` gives the four-cell frame (one pixel outside the brush, cross through the handle), clipped at the near plane; `slide` and `snap` move the brush on its plane and on the perspective grid.
  - **No new Command.** A stamp and a FillScreen are one `Command::Stroke` each with the projected brush as custom brush (handle 0,0): one undo step, the current mode, ink and stencil apply as for any brush stamp. Replace mode is emulated: the brush's transparent pixels become BG inside the brush and the result is stamped Matte, so the area around the turned brush stays untouched; with the right button the whole turned brush is BG (our Replace paints the brush rectangle in BG with the right button, ADR-18). When the brush's colours and BG take the whole palette (any brush on a 2-colour screen with BG 0, a 4-colour brush on a 4-colour screen), no colour is left to stand for "nothing": then the pixels around the turned brush repeat the picture under it and the stamp has no transparent colour (still one Stroke; the effects leave an unchanged colour as it is).
  - **The mode (`apps/deluxepainter/src/perspective.rs`).** Do (menu or Enter) needs a custom brush; Do and FillScreen are ghosted without one (Center, Reset and Settings.. are always on). A click on the page stamps; a click on a drawing tool, the Brush Selector, Text or a built-in brush, a new pick-up or load, or no brush ends the mode. UNDO, CLR, Grid, Symmetry, Magnify and Zoom do their job and keep it (they choose no tool; CLR p.131). A handle or flip change keeps it (Alt-x, p.142); so do the Brush menu gestures (Handle ▸ Place, Stretch, Rotate ▸ Any Angle, Shear, Bend): while one is armed the page click belongs to it and its flat preview replaces the wire frame, then the mode goes on with the new brush. The title bar shows the angles (or the position) as three 4-wide numbers; Prefs ▸ Coords, when on, keeps showing coordinates instead. The requester's settings apply on Ok; Brush → Screen rounds the matrix back to whole angles.
  - **Keys** (main keys stand in for the keypad, see Context): Enter toggles the mode (not while the Text tool types). Only inside the mode: `7`/`8` X, `4`/`5` Y, `1`/`2` Z by 1° (Shift by the Angle Step; the digit is read from the physical key, so Shift-digits work on any layout and the typed character is swallowed); `9`/`6`/`3` reset an axis, Shift fixes it; `0` resets the angles, Shift-`0` also the depth, the settings and the distance; `-` FillScreen and `.` Center, both read from the typed character, so Shift-. is `>` (US, UK) or `:` (German) and not Center; `;` `'` 4 px, `:` `"` `@` 32 px along the fixed axis (Grid on: that axis's grid step), and also by the key's place: the keys where a US keyboard has `;` and `'` step (Shift: 32) whatever they type (German Ö Ä, UK Shift-' = `@`), unless egui names them as a letter or another key (French M, Dvorak S and `-`); `<` `>` distance ×0.8 / ×1.25 (32..16384); `\` angles / position; held down, the turns, steps and `<` `>` repeat, `-` `.` `\` do not; `G` (as outside the mode) toggles Grid with the brush handle as a grid point, which becomes the perspective grid's origin. Escape cancels a Center placing.
  - **Showing it.** Before the screen is composed, a copy of the page gets the wire frame and the centre's cross hair in complementary colours (`colours − 1 − c`); the brush image itself is drawn only while it lies unturned on the screen plane (the manual shows the wire frame while turning). Center shows a cross hair across the page until a click places the centre.
  - **With the other wave-7 features** (review of the merge):
    - Area stencil (ADR-39): the unturned brush image shown in the mode is made like the click's stamp, wrapped in `Stenciled` (`with_area_stencil`), so it goes behind the stencil exactly where the click spares the picture.
    - Stencil ▸ Paint in the mode: the stencil view (dimmed picture, mask bright) shows the brush where a click stamps it into the mask, and the wire frame and cross hairs are drawn on top of that view in the complementary colours of what it shows (`draw_perspective_marks` runs after `stencil::view`). A click then stamps into the mask; the picture stays.
    - Which changes end the mode: the app counts brush pick-ups and Brush ▸ Load.. (`ToolState::brush_gen`), and the mode ends when that count moved, no longer when the brush's pixels differ. Color ▸ Palette ▸ Arrange and Use Brush Palette renumber the brush in place and keep the mode, as the handle and flip changes do.
    - AnimBrush (ADR-36): a click and a FillScreen paint the current cel and move the clock on by one.
    - Anim ▸ Move (ADR-38): a click records the stamp's exact pose (position, depth and orientation) as the start of the next move, and Move projects with the same ray casting (`Placement::local`) and the mode's centre and distance, so a move of no distance repeats the stamp pixel for pixel.
  - **Fill Type Perspective is not wired.** `FillType` is a small `Copy` value inside the serialized `FloodFill` command (CLI/MCP JSON); a perspective fill needs the custom brush and the perspective state, i.e. a command format change, and the Fill Type requester (ADR-20) still ghosts Brush and Wrap too. The building block exists (`fill_plane` with an area); the button stays ghosted.
  - **Headless snapshots.** `--perspective-requester` renders the requester; `--perspective 'center=X,Y;distance=D;fill:AX,AY,AZ@X,Y[,Z];stamp:...'` (with `--pick`) replays stamps and fills through the engine.
  - **Decided without the owner** (visible, picked as the most DeluxePaint-like):
    - Keypad keys are the main keys; outside the mode only Enter is taken, digits keep their animation meaning there.
    - The turned brush shows as wire frame only; its image only unturned on the screen plane.
    - A click stamps once on press; dragging does not paint a trail.
    - FillScreen keeps the mode on; it fills the visible screen part of the page.
    - Reset from the menu keeps the centre (the manual's note is ambiguous); the first Do puts the centre in the middle of the screen.
    - Default observer distance = screen width; steps 4 / 32 px; distance factor 1.25.
    - The perspective grid snaps along the brush's axes from a grid origin (`G` sets it); AutoGrid follows the brush size.
    - Wire frame and cross hairs in complementary colours.
    - The requester uses our radio buttons for Type and Display (DeluxePaint: toggle buttons); From Brush is ghosted without a brush.
    - UNDO, Grid, Symmetry, Magnify and Zoom keep the mode like CLR does (the manual shows only CLR); every button that chooses a tool or a brush ends it.
    - Brush menu gestures work in the mode and show their flat preview meanwhile.
    - `;` `'` (Shift: the large step) also by the key's place, and `@` as the large step away, so that every step has a key on German and UK keyboards (on a German keyboard `"` is Shift-2, a keypad turn here).
    - Replace with no colour to spare repeats the picture around the brush; AntiAlias then does not soften the brush's edge against the picture.
    - Perspective state (angles, centre, settings) is not saved: not in `.dpxproj`, not in the settings file, no `DPPS` (ADR-12).
    - Wave-7 review: the brush shown in the mode goes behind an area stencil; during Stencil ▸ Paint the brush shows in the mask with the marks on top; Arrange and Use Brush Palette keep the mode (only a pick-up or a load ends it); an AnimBrush goes on one cel per click and per FillScreen; Move starts from the last stamp's pose. Visible.
- Verified:
  - `cargo fmt --all -- --check`, `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace` green.
  - Unit tests (dpx-core): sine table and angle normalisation, Euler matrices and their decomposition, Type Brush turns, projection of an unturned brush (pixel-exact), quarter turns, perspective shrink with depth, near/far clipping, Replace emulation, also on full 2- and 4-colour palettes with the left and right button (executed as Stroke commands through the engine, with Translucency, Process and AntiAlias leaving the picture around the brush unchanged), plane fill up to the horizon, wire frame and cross hair (every pixel once; the page-wide cross on a 4096×4096 page well under 20 ms, the old duplicate check took about 200 ms), slide and snap; two FNV hashes pin a turned stamp and a floor fill.
  - Unit tests (dpx-ui): requester defaults, From Brush (and ghosted without a brush), Ok settings; title bar angles vs. Coords; menu ghosting of Do / FillScreen.
  - App tests drive `PainterApp::ui` with real egui key, text, modifier and pointer events: Do from the menu and Enter; turning by 1° and by the Angle Step (Shift-digits as typed on US and German layouts); axis resets and fixes; `;` `'` `:` `"`, `<` `>`, `\`; Shift-. as `>` (US) and `:` (German) with the key event egui sends, not Center; a held `-` fills once; the step keys by place (German Ö Ä with and without Shift, UK `@`, US Shift-; once, French M not); digits do not step animation frames in the mode; a click stamps one undoable command (pixel checks, also after a quarter turn); a tool click ends the mode, UNDO, CLR, Grid and Symmetry clicks keep it; Brush ▸ Handle ▸ Place in the mode moves the handle without a stamp and the mode stamps with it; Replace on a 4-colour screen (left: BG inside the brush; right, turned: only the brush, not its box); right click on Grid opens the Perspective requester in the mode (Gridding outside), Tab/typing/radio/Return apply Angle Step and Type; Settings.. from the menu, Escape cancels; FillScreen (floor up to the horizon, one undo step) from the key and the menu; Center with Escape and click; Reset and Shift-0 vs. 0; the composed screen shows the brush, frame, cross and centre and drops the copy after drawing; the mouse slides the brush, Ctrl fixes Y, Grid snaps it; no brush / Text typing; a new pick-up or no brush ends the mode, a handle change does not; the `--perspective` spec.
  - Wave-7 review fixes, each app test failing with its fix taken out again (through `PainterApp::ui` with real egui events): `the_brush_preview_goes_behind_an_area_stencil_as_the_click_does` (the composed screen spares the protected pixel and the click paints exactly what was shown); `stencil_paint_in_the_mode_shows_the_brush_in_the_mask_and_the_marks_on_top` (Ctrl-s in the mode, dimmed picture, brush bright, wire frame and centre in complementary colours; the click stamps the mask only; fails both without the mask preview and without the marks); `arrange_and_use_brush_palette_keep_perspective_mode` (Arrange and Use Brush Palette called on the app, then `ui()` passes: still in the mode); `a_move_after_a_perspective_stamp_starts_from_the_turned_brush` (Z 90°, X −1°, pushed 8 pixels, click, `U`, Shift-M, Return with Count 1: the page equals the stamp; fails without the recorded pose and without the view in the command); `perspective_clicks_with_an_animbrush_go_on_cel_by_cel` (ADR-36).
  - Looked at headless renders: the requester, a fill-and-stamps snapshot (floor to the horizon, turned and distant stamps) and the in-mode screen (wire frame, centre cross, `-40 25 10` in the title bar).
  - Not verified: a real window with a real keyboard (keypad keys on macOS, Shift-digits and the key places of `;` `'` on other layouts; the tests send the key, place and text that egui-winit 0.37 reports according to its source) and mouse; the look against the original program side by side.
