# ADR-37: Effect ▸ Perspective

- Status: accepted (Phase 4, 2026-11-11; decided without the owner where marked, owner questions listed at the end)
- Context:
  - DeluxePaint IV AGA turns the custom brush in 3D and paints it in perspective: Effect ▸ Perspective ▸ Do (keypad Enter), FillScreen (keypad `-`), Reset (keypad `0`), Center (keypad `.`) and Settings.. (also a right click on Grid while the mode is on). In the mode the brush is a wire frame of four cells, the keypad turns it about X, Y and Z (Shift: by the Angle Step), the mouse slides it on its plane, `;` `'` move it along its fixed axis, `<` `>` move the observer, Ctrl fixes Y, `\` switches the title bar between angles and position; a click paints it; clicking a tool leaves the mode (tutorial AGA p.126–145, menu p.278, requester p.279–281, title bar p.286, keys p.295).
  - The Perspective requester: Grid X / Y / Z (brush size, Z = Y), From Brush, Angle Step (90), Type Screen (Euler) / Brush, Display Angle / Pos, Cancel, Ok (p.279–281). Fill Type also has a Perspective fill (p.211).
  - egui 0.37 cannot tell the keypad from the main keys: egui-winit maps `Numpad0`–`Numpad9` to `Num0`–`Num9`, keypad Enter to Enter, keypad `-` / `.` to Minus / Period, and drops the key location. Main-row `1`–`6` are animation keys (ADR-28).
  - Rule: no pixel logic outside `dpx-core`/`dpx-raster`; one Command engine for GUI, CLI and MCP.
- Decision:
  - **Core (`dpx-core::perspective`).** Page pixels are x right, y down, z into the screen; the screen is the plane z = 0, the observer stands `distance` in front of the centre. Orientation is a 3×3 matrix (columns: the brush's axes). Type Screen keeps Euler angles, M = Rx·Ry·Rz; Type Brush turns about the brush's own axes and reads the angles back out of the matrix. Sines come from a table of whole degrees (exact at 0/30/45/60/90), so every result is deterministic. Projection is inverse ray casting: each page pixel's centre ray meets the brush plane, the hit is mapped to the nearest brush pixel; rays nearer than 1 pixel of depth or beyond 64 × distance (planes only) paint nothing. Results are trimmed brush images placed at their top-left corner. `fill_plane` tiles a whole plane by the grid; `wireframe` gives the four-cell frame (one pixel outside the brush, cross through the handle), clipped at the near plane; `slide` and `snap` move the brush on its plane and on the perspective grid.
  - **No new Command.** A stamp and a FillScreen are one `Command::Stroke` each with the projected brush as custom brush (handle 0,0): one undo step, the current mode, ink and stencil apply as for any brush stamp. Replace mode is emulated: the brush's transparent pixels become BG inside the brush and the result is stamped Matte, so the area around the turned brush stays untouched.
  - **The mode (`apps/deluxepainter/src/perspective.rs`).** Do (menu or Enter) needs a custom brush; Do and FillScreen are ghosted without one (Center, Reset and Settings.. are always on). A click on the page stamps; a click on a tool or a brush in the toolbox, a new pick-up or load, or no brush ends the mode; a handle or flip change keeps it (the tutorial uses Alt-x in the mode, p.142). The title bar shows the angles (or the position) as three 4-wide numbers; Prefs ▸ Coords, when on, keeps showing coordinates instead. The requester's settings apply on Ok; Brush → Screen rounds the matrix back to whole angles.
  - **Keys** (main keys stand in for the keypad, see Context): Enter toggles the mode (not while the Text tool types). Only inside the mode: `7`/`8` X, `4`/`5` Y, `1`/`2` Z by 1° (Shift by the Angle Step; the digit is read from the physical key, so Shift-digits work on any layout and the typed character is swallowed); `9`/`6`/`3` reset an axis, Shift fixes it; `0` resets the angles, Shift-`0` also the depth, the settings and the distance; `-` FillScreen; `.` Center; `;` `'` 4 px, `:` `"` 32 px along the fixed axis (Grid on: that axis's grid step); `<` `>` distance ×0.8 / ×1.25 (32..16384); `\` angles / position; `G` (as outside the mode) toggles Grid with the brush handle as a grid point, which becomes the perspective grid's origin. Escape cancels a Center placing.
  - **Showing it.** Before the screen is composed, a copy of the page gets the wire frame and the centre's cross hair in complementary colours (`colours − 1 − c`); the brush image itself is drawn only while it lies unturned on the screen plane (the manual shows the wire frame while turning). Center shows a cross hair across the page until a click places the centre.
  - **Fill Type Perspective is not wired.** `FillType` is a small `Copy` value inside the serialized `FloodFill` command (CLI/MCP JSON); a perspective fill needs the custom brush and the perspective state, i.e. a command format change, and the Fill Type requester (ADR-20) still ghosts Brush and Wrap too. The building block exists (`fill_plane` with an area); the button stays ghosted.
  - **Headless snapshots.** `--perspective-requester` renders the requester; `--perspective 'center=X,Y;distance=D;fill:AX,AY,AZ@X,Y[,Z];stamp:...'` (with `--pick`) replays stamps and fills through the engine.
  - **Decided without the owner** (visible, picked as the most DeluxePaint-like):
    - Keypad keys are the main keys; outside the mode only Enter is taken, digits keep their animation meaning there.
    - The turned brush shows as wire frame only; its image only unturned on the screen plane.
    - A click stamps once on press; dragging does not paint a trail.
    - FillScreen keeps the mode on; it fills the visible screen part of the page.
    - Reset from the menu keeps the centre (the manual's note is ambiguous); the first Do puts the centre in the middle of the screen.
    - Default observer distance = screen width; steps 4 / 32 px; distance factor 1.25.
    - The perspective grid snaps along the brush's axes from a grid origin (`G` sets it); AutoGrid follows the brush size.
    - Wire frame and cross hairs in complementary colours.
    - The requester uses our radio buttons for Type and Display (DeluxePaint: toggle buttons); From Brush is ghosted without a brush.
    - Perspective state (angles, centre, settings) is not saved: not in `.dpxproj`, not in the settings file, no `DPPS` (ADR-12).
- Verified:
  - `cargo fmt --all -- --check`, `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace` green.
  - Unit tests (dpx-core): sine table and angle normalisation, Euler matrices and their decomposition, Type Brush turns, projection of an unturned brush (pixel-exact), quarter turns, perspective shrink with depth, near/far clipping, Replace emulation, plane fill up to the horizon, wire frame and cross hair, slide and snap; two FNV hashes pin a turned stamp and a floor fill.
  - Unit tests (dpx-ui): requester defaults, From Brush (and ghosted without a brush), Ok settings; title bar angles vs. Coords; menu ghosting of Do / FillScreen.
  - App tests drive `PainterApp::ui` with real egui key, text, modifier and pointer events: Do from the menu and Enter; turning by 1° and by the Angle Step (Shift-digits as typed on US and German layouts); axis resets and fixes; `;` `'` `:` `"`, `<` `>`, `\`; digits do not step animation frames in the mode; a click stamps one undoable command (pixel checks, also after a quarter turn); a tool click ends the mode; right click on Grid opens the Perspective requester in the mode (Gridding outside), Tab/typing/radio/Return apply Angle Step and Type; Settings.. from the menu, Escape cancels; FillScreen (floor up to the horizon, one undo step) from the key and the menu; Center with Escape and click; Reset and Shift-0 vs. 0; the composed screen shows the brush, frame, cross and centre and drops the copy after drawing; the mouse slides the brush, Ctrl fixes Y, Grid snaps it; no brush / Text typing; a new pick-up or no brush ends the mode, a handle change does not; the `--perspective` spec.
