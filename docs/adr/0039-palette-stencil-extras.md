# ADR-39: Use Brush Palette, Recompute, Stencil ▸ Paint and Background ▸ Lock FG

- Status: accepted (wave 7, 2026-11-11; the points under "Decided without the owner" were not asked)
- Context:
  - The last ghosted Color and Effect items: Color ▸ Palette ▸ Use Brush Palette, Color ▸ Recompute, Effect ▸ Stencil ▸ Paint (Ctrl-s), Effect ▸ Background ▸ Lock FG, and Picture ▸ Print.
  - Manual (AGA): Use Brush Palette switches the picture to the brush's palette with the brush's cycling information; a brush palette with more colours than the picture is recomputed with the picture's number; Restore Palette goes back (p.267–268). Recompute is for HAM only: it redoes the picture data by a remap with the current palette and reduces the fringing of HAM pictures from other programs (p.270). Stencil ▸ Paint (Ctrl-s) paints the area to protect with the current brush, shows the image in a "half-tone" mode, ends when chosen again; painted stencils are area based, apply to the current picture or frame only and are discarded by Make or Remake (p.271–273). Lock FG makes a stencil of the areas painted since Background ▸ Fix, whatever their colour (p.275). The stencil tutorial adds: colour and area stencils exclude each other, CLR does not clear the stencilled area, Stencil Paint starts from the colours selected in the Make Stencil requester, the left button adds, the right one subtracts, the title shows a lower-case `s`, and other options may end the mode (p.119–120).
  - The colour stencil of ADR-20/31 is a 256-entry table that strokes and shapes carry; there was no area stencil, and brushes kept no palette of their own (ADR-19 fits loaded brushes to the picture palette).
- Decision:
  - **Area stencil in `dpx-core` (`stencil.rs`)**: `AreaStencil` is a page-sized mask (shared by clones; JSON as run lengths). The new `Command::Stenciled { area, command }` (at the end of the command enum) covers a Stroke, Shape, FloodFill, Clear, or a SetPage of the page's size: strokes and shapes test every pixel against the area as they paint (`PaintContext::area`, the same test as the colour stencil in `paint::plot`), fills, CLR and SetPage paint first and put the protected pixels back. One undo step; redo replays it; the GUI, CLI and MCP send the same command.
  - **Wiring (`apps/deluxepainter/src/palette_stencil.rs`)**: the tools wrap every command of a gesture (`Ink::area`), so the preview spares exactly what the release spares (the custom brush goes behind the stencil while it hovers, tutorial p.119). `run`/`execute` wrap fills and CLR, and CLR on a fixed background (the SetPage of ADR-15), which therefore keeps the locked pixels (tutorial p.119).
  - **Stencil ▸ Paint** (menu or Ctrl-s): the mask lives in its own two-colour `Engine`; every painting command (the tools, Fill, CLR, the Brush Selector's cut) goes to it through `stencil::mask_command`: left adds, right frees, a custom brush paints its shape, Fill fills the mask's region at the point (FG adds, BG frees), CLR empties the mask. It starts from the area stencil of this frame, else from the pixels in the colours of the colour stencil (on or off), else empty. Choosing it again turns the mask into the area stencil (an empty mask: no stencil). Undo and Redo step through the stencil strokes while it is on.
  - **View and flag**: during Paint, and during Show with an area stencil, the picture is shown dimmed and the protected pixels at full brightness (`stencil::view`); the title bar shows `s` then, `S` while a stencil applies.
  - **Colour and area stencils exclude each other**: making an area stencil puts the colour stencil away into the off slot (its colours stay the Make Stencil selection, so Make and Remake start from them); making a colour stencil (Make Ok, Remake, Load) discards the area stencil. With an area stencil, Show, Reverse, On/Off and Free act on it, Remake discards it and remakes the colour stencil, Save.. says a painted stencil cannot be saved yet.
  - **Frames and size**: an area stencil applies on the frame it was made on only (it is kept for that frame meanwhile) and is dropped when the page size changes; Spare ▸ Swap and loading a picture of the same size keep it (one stencil for the screen, like the colour stencil).
  - **Lock FG**: the pixels that differ from the fixed background. Without a fixed background, or with nothing changed, a note says so.
  - **Use Brush Palette** (enabled with a custom brush, like the brush items): the brush palette is the brush file's CMAP and CRNG for a loaded brush (Brush ▸ Load.. now keeps them; painting still uses the fitted brush of ADR-19) and the picture palette of the pick-up moment for a picked-up brush (no ranges). The loaded brush unchanged (Restore included) uses the file's colour numbers exactly; after a change its colours are fitted to the nearest numbers of the file palette. If the brush's numbers fit the picture's colour count, the palette is taken as it is (entries the brush palette lacks keep the picture's colours, a longer one is cut) and the brush keeps its numbers. Otherwise `dpx_core::brush_palette` recomputes the palette from the brush's pixels (`quantize::reduce`: exact when few enough, else median cut; transparent in colour 0; 12-bit on OCS), fills the entries left with the brush palette's other colours, repaints the brush, and leaves the ranges out with a note. The palette change is one undoable `SetPalette`; the picture keeps its colour numbers, so it may look different, and Color ▸ Remap can fit it afterwards. The file's ranges replace the picture's (inside the palette), as Palette ▸ Load does. Brush ▸ Save.. writes the picture's ranges as CRNG.
  - **Recompute**: in the menu model, enabled only on HAM screens (`MenuContext::ham`). No document is HAM (ADR-26 reduces HAM pictures on load), so the item stays ghosted; reached anyway, it says it works on HAM pictures only. The HAM work exists as `dpx_formats::ham::recompute`: the data decoded with the old base palette and encoded again for the new one (the encoder of ADR-25), ready for HAM editing.
  - **Print stays ghosted** (out of scope): DeluxePainter's outputs are fixed to `.dpxproj`, IFF ILBM, IFF ANIM and PNG (CLAUDE.md) and it has no printer path; an exported PNG can be printed with the system's tools. Nothing in the manual depends on printing.
  - Snapshot option `--stencil-paint X,Y,W,H`: the Stencil Paint view with that rectangle painted.
- Decided without the owner:
  - Dimming takes the nearest palette colour at half brightness: the screen has one palette, so colours without a darker neighbour in it stay alike (the manual's "half-tone" look is not known in detail). Visible.
  - Any other menu item ends Stencil ▸ Paint and keeps what was painted; Undo/Redo act on the stencil while it is on; Fill, CLR and the Brush Selector's cut paint the mask as described above.
  - Area stencils are kept per frame and dropped on a page-size change; Spare ▸ Swap and loads of the same size keep them.
  - Lock FG counts a pixel repainted in its old colour as unpainted (the fixed background is compared, nothing records strokes); without a fixed background the item gives a note instead of being ghosted.
  - Use Brush Palette's handling of changed brushes, shorter and longer brush palettes and ranges as above; brush files now carry CRNG.
- Deviations from DeluxePaint IV: painted stencils cannot be saved (the stencil file of ADR-31 holds colours); Undo of Use Brush Palette restores the palette but not the brush or the ranges (DeluxePaint has one undo level and Restore Palette); fills and CLR honour the area stencil, but still not the colour stencil (unchanged from ADR-20); a stencil does not yet make protected pixels transparent when a brush is picked up (AGA p.271).
- Not stored: the area stencil is not part of the `.dpxproj` project (neither is the colour stencil).
- Verified:
  - Unit tests (`dpx-core`): mask JSON round trip and refusals, masks from colours, from changes (Lock FG), reversed, restore, the dimmed view, mask commands (strokes, custom brush shape, fill, CLR, nested), `Stenciled` strokes/CLR/fills/SetPage in the engine with undo and redo, the engine result equal to the preview, validation (size, kinds, nesting, colours), command JSON; Use Brush Palette (fits, cut, recomputed with exact and quantised colours, transparent kept, OCS precision), brush fitting that avoids the transparent colour. `dpx-formats`: Recompute keeps the look under a changed base palette and changes nothing under the same one.
  - App tests through `PainterApp::ui` with real egui events: Ctrl-s on and off, left click adds, right click frees, `u` undoes in the stencil, the uploaded screen shows the dimmed picture with the stencil bright, the title flags `s` and `S`, painting goes around the stencil; the hover preview of a custom brush goes behind it and equals the committed result; Stencil ▸ Paint and Lock FG picked from the menu strip with the right button; Lock FG notes, protection and CLR on the fixed background; Use Brush Palette from the menu with a brush file written and loaded through the file requester (palette, brush numbers, ranges, Restore, Remap base, one undo step), a recomputed palette, a picked-up brush; stencil items on an area stencil (Reverse, Show disables painting, On/Off, Save note, Free), a colour stencil discarding it, frame and page-size rules, ghosting of all five items.
  - Mutation check: removing the area test in `plot`, the tools' wrapping, the CLR-on-background wrapping, the undo hook, the discard by a colour stencil, ending Paint on other items, the exact loaded-brush path, the restore after fills, the view hook, the Ctrl-s hook or the refresh after an item each made a test fail (eleven mutations).
  - Headless snapshot with `--stencil-paint` looked at; CI gate (fmt, clippy `-D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace`) on macOS.
- Not verified: by hand in the window or with a real keyboard; DeluxePaint's exact stencil display and its handling of tools during Stencil Paint; Recompute against real HAM files (the app never has a HAM picture).
