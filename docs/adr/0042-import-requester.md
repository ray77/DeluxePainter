# ADR-42: The Import requester for PNG and JPEG pictures

- Status: accepted (2026-11-11, owner request "Beim Import muss mehr gefragt werden. Mittig, gezoomt, auf wie viele Farben konvertieren etc" – "Import must ask more: centred, zoomed, how many colours to convert to etc."; the visible behaviour was decided by the owner, the points under "Decided without the owner" were not asked)
- Context:
  - ADR-9 opened a true-colour PNG or JPEG without asking: a picture that fits a screen format got its own screen (mode guessed from its size), a bigger one was scaled down to the current screen; the palette always had the mode's full colour count, the dither was always Floyd–Steinberg, and a smaller picture sat top-left on colour 0. Indexed PNGs kept their palette and got a screen of their own.
  - DeluxePaint IV AGA reads only IFF (it converts 24-bit IFF to the current mode, AGA p.29), so there is no original requester to follow; this is a DeluxePainter addition in DeluxePaint's look.
- Decision:
  - **When:** the Import Picture requester appears only when Picture ▸ Load.. picks a PNG (indexed or true colour) or a JPEG (`files::Picture::Png`, and `Picture::TrueColor` with the format PNG or JPEG). IFF ILBM (HAM ILBMs included) / ANIM and `.dpxproj` load as before without questions, and so does anything Anim ▸ Load.. picks. `--open`, the CLI (`info`, `convert`) and MCP `open_picture` keep ADR-9 unchanged (no requester, a screen of its own): only the GUI's Picture ▸ Load.. asks.
  - **Size ("fit, zoomed"):** the picture is always fitted into the current document's page (the screen, or the larger page Picture ▸ Page Size made) keeping its proportions on the screen's pixel shape (`resample::fit_size`), enlarged as well as reduced. Display mode, chipset, screen and page size stay. Scaling is per axis (`resample::scale_fit`): an axis that grows (or keeps its size) takes the nearest source pixel, so pixel art stays crisp and gets no new colours; an axis that shrinks averages areas with premultiplied alpha (`scale_area`, ADR-9). The fitting is done once when the requester opens; the fitted picture is kept and option changes only reduce it again.
  - **Position:** Centre / Top left (default Centre). Centring puts an odd pixel of margin right and below. The rest of the page is the current BG colour.
  - **Palette and colours:** New (ADR-9's median cut + k-means, rounded to the chipset's palette depth, exact when the picture has no more colours) with 2, 4, 8 … 256 entries up to the screen's maximum (`DisplayMode::max_colors`: OCS LoRes 32, HiRes 16, AGA 256); default the current document's palette size in whole bitplanes (a 12-entry palette, as an indexed PNG or a short ILBM CMAP gives, takes 4 bitplanes: 16; never fewer colours than the document has). The new palette has exactly that many entries (padded with black, whole bitplanes). Current keeps the current palette exactly and maps every pixel to the nearest of its colours with ADR-9's weighted distance (`quantize::reduce_to_palette`); the colour count is ghosted then and shows the current palette's size. A picture made only of palette colours maps exactly (no dither), as New keeps few colours exactly.
  - **Dither:** None / Floyd–Steinberg (default, ADR-9) / Ordered. Ordered uses the **4×4** Bayer matrix: it is the matrix of the gradient fills' dither (ADR-20), so both dithers in the program look alike, and on LoRes pixels its 4-pixel cell is a finer, less visible texture than the 8×8 matrix's; 16 levels between two neighbouring colours are enough for palettes of 2–256 colours. It mixes two colours per pixel: the nearest palette colour and the nearest one of another colour (ADR-9's weighted distance), taken in index order; `f` is the pixel's place on the line between them (its weighted projection, 0 at the first, 1 at the second), and the pixel takes the second colour where `f` is above the cell's threshold `(t + ½) / 16`, so `f` of the 16 cells of every 4×4 block show it. A pixel that is a palette colour never changes (`f` is 0 or 1), so flat areas of palette colours stay clean as with None and Floyd–Steinberg, and the mix follows any direction between two colours (a blue-to-orange ramp dithers as well as a grey ramp). (Amended after the review: the first version moved every pixel along the grey axis by a palette-wide mean colour step, which flipped up to a quarter of the pixels of flat black and white areas and barely dithered hue ramps.)
  - **Margins and dithering:** the BG colour's RGB counts for a new palette like that many picture pixels (in the histogram and the palette order). A picture whose own colours fit into the count keeps them exactly (as the spec requires for indexed PNGs, also enlarged); the margin then gets an entry of its own while there is room, else the nearest picture colour (`quantize::reduce_with`; the margin never pushes a picture with few colours into quantizing and dithering). Only a picture with more colours than the count is quantized, with the margin in the histogram. With the current palette the margin is the BG index exactly (also when the palette holds the same colour at a lower index). Margins take no part in the dithering, so no dither noise reaches the page edge.
  - **Transparency (not a requester option):** ADR-9 stays: with New, pixels with alpha < 128 become colour 0, reserved and pink; with Current they become colour 0 of the current palette, which opaque pixels then do not use (as with New); in both cases the document's transparent colour is 0. A margin in BG colour 0 is then transparent in an ILBM save too, like DeluxePaint's background.
  - **Preview:** the requester shows a thumbnail of the result made from exactly the page Ok takes over (`import::thumbnail`: that page in its palette's colours, area-averaged into the box). Every option change makes it again. **Deliberate deviation:** the screen is indexed with one palette, which cannot show a new palette's colours, so the thumbnail is the only part of the screen painted in true colour: the requester leaves its box dark, and after the indexed screen has become RGBA, `ImportRequester::overlay` (`dpx_ui::overlay_rgb`) writes the thumbnail into the frame at the box, scaled by the screen's UI scale (`ux`/`uy`) and, for `--snapshot` PNGs, the snapshot scale. Both the window texture and `--snapshot` do this. The overlay is left out while the quit question (choice requester) is on top.
  - **Speed (amended after the review):** an option change must not block. The picture is fitted once when the requester opens (`import::Fitted`); a new palette is built once per colour count (it does not depend on the dither or the position), a dither change only maps the picture again, and a position change only places the same indexed picture again. Nearest colours are found through a 16×16×16 grid of RGB cells, each keeping the palette entries that can be nearest (or nearest of another colour) for some point in it; it gives exactly the plain search's answers (ties to the lowest index), so ADR-9's results (`--open`, CLI, MCP, HAM, brush palettes) do not change. Fitted pictures of 2 megapixels or more (only pages made larger with Page Size; the largest standard screen is 1280×512) are reduced on a background thread: the click returns at once, the thumbnail follows when the page is made, only the newest options are computed, and Ok waits for the page of exactly the requester's options. Open (fit and the first page) stays synchronous.
  - **Requester (`dpx_ui::import_requester`):** title "Import Picture"; a line with the file name, its size and colours ("photo.jpg  4032x3024  true colour", "sprite.png  40x30  7 colours"; above 256 colours "true colour"; a long name is cut) and the size on the page ("-> 320x240 on 320x256"); Position, Palette, Colours (◂ count ▶, stops at 2 and at the screen's maximum), Dither, the thumbnail box right of the options, Cancel and Ok. Square corners, palette pens, the 7×7 radio boxes of the tool requesters (ADR-20). Its size follows the UI screen (screen − 8, between 288×150 and 360×232), so it fits 320×200 (NTSC LoRes and every other screen's smallest size) and the thumbnail grows on bigger screens; HiRes, SuperHiRes and laced screens draw it through `render_scaled` like every requester. Radios act on press, buttons on release. Return = Ok, Esc = Cancel; only a new press of Return answers it: the auto-repeat of a held Return (the press that picked the file in the file requester) is left out (`modal_events_with`), so the picture is never replaced before the requester was seen. The focused option's label is highlighted like a menu item (a bar in the select pen, the text in another pen), which shows on every palette, also with 2 and 4 colours where the select pen is the dark one; a ghosted row is ghosted whole (label too, as a dot pattern without a ghost pen) and shows no focus (choosing Current moves the focus from Colours to Palette).
  - **Modal:** `PainterApp::modal_open` includes it; it gets the pointer and keys before the paint screen (its branch comes right after the quit question's), the colour-cycling clock keeps running under it (ADR-35), the menu strip is closed and a stroke or the custom brush's hover preview is dropped when it opens. It is the first requester that covers the toolbox column, so the toolbox marks drawn after the screen is composed (Grid, Symmetry and Magnify switched on, the custom brush's "no built-in brush" mark) stay under it (`import::under_import_requester`, window and `--snapshot`). The file requester closes when it opens, so Cancel leaves both closed and the old picture untouched.
  - **Ok:** the page becomes the picture like Load (`set_picture`: not undoable, ADR-8 unchanged; the autosave and recovery rules, the cycling reset and Restore Palette's palette follow). Notes: none for what the user chose (scaling, colour count, dither); the file's own notes stay (e.g. an indexed PNG's ignored tRNS entries), plus "colours rounded to 12-bit OCS values" when exact colours had to be rounded and the transparency note.
  - **Remembered:** the last Position, Palette, Colours and Dither (taken at Ok) for the session and in `settings.json` (ADR-33) under `import` (`position` centre/top_left, `palette` new/current, `colors`, `dither` none/floyd_steinberg/ordered). Missing fields keep the defaults, so older files load; `colors` is made a whole bitplane count in 2–256 when read and limited to the screen's maximum when the requester opens; until an import was made it is absent and the document's palette size is used.
  - **Code:** pixel logic in `dpx-core` (`import`: `fit`, `Fitted`, `place`, `reduce_fitted`, `import`, `thumbnail`, `rgba_of`, `count_colors`; `quantize`: `Dither::Ordered`, `reduce_with` = `build_palette` + `map_built`, `reduce_to_palette`; `resample`: `scale_nearest`, `scale_fit`); the requester in `dpx-ui`; the app part in `apps/deluxepainter/src/import.rs` (`main.rs` only routes Load, adds the modal branch and the two overlays). Indexed PNGs go the same way: they become RGBA in their palette's colours first.
  - **Snapshot options:** `--import-requester FILE` shows the requester for a PNG or JPEG over the current screen (default, `--open` or `--page`), with its thumbnail; `--import 'position=centre|top_left;palette=new|current;colors=N;dither=none|fs|ordered'` sets options (any keys, any order); `--import-apply` shows the page after Ok instead. Bad keys or values, colour counts that are no power of two or above the screen's maximum, a file that is no PNG/JPEG, or `--import`/`--import-apply` without `--import-requester` exit with code 2; an unreadable file with code 1.
- Owner decisions (2026-11-11, answers to the follow-up questions, binding):
  - When: "only when Picture > Load.. picks a PNG (indexed or true colour) or JPEG file. IFF ILBM / ANIM and .dpxproj load as before without questions (DeluxePaint behaviour)."
  - Size: "Fit (zoom) only: the picture is always fitted into the current document's page … keeping its proportions on the current screen's pixel shape, enlarging as well as reducing. The current display mode, chipset, screen size and page size are kept" (not "own screen", "1:1" or "fill/stretch").
  - Position: "Centre / Top left (default Centre). The part of the page the picture does not cover gets the current BG colour (its RGB takes part in the reduction like any picture pixel, so with Current palette it is the BG index exactly)."
  - Colours: "2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256 up to the current screen's maximum; default = the current document's palette size. The new document's palette has that many entries (whole bitplanes)."
  - Palette: "New (median cut + k-means as ADR-9, rounded to the chipset's palette depth) or Current (keep the current document's palette exactly, every pixel mapped to the nearest of its colours with the same weighted distance as ADR-9). With Current the colour count is ghosted."
  - Dither: "None / Floyd-Steinberg / Ordered (Bayer). Default Floyd-Steinberg."
  - Transparency: "not a requester option; keep ADR-9 (alpha < 128 → colour 0 reserved pink with New; with Current: transparent pixels become colour 0 of the current palette and the document's transparent colour is 0)."
  - Preview: "a thumbnail of the result inside the requester (the page stays unchanged until Ok); every option change recomputes it; it must show the true colours of the result even with a new palette" – the RGB overlay above.
  - Margin when the picture uses every colour of the chosen count (asked after the review, answer "So lassen", i.e. keep it): the picture stays exact and the margin takes the nearest picture colour; no entry is reserved for the BG colour.
- Decided without the owner:
  - Bayer 4×4 rather than 8×8, and the palette-adaptive spread (above).
  - Per-axis scaling: nearest neighbour where an axis grows, area averaging where it shrinks (pictures can grow on one axis and shrink on the other on non-square pixel shapes).
  - Margins take no part in dithering; with Current the margin is the BG index even if a lower index has the same colour.
  - With Current and transparent pixels, opaque pixels avoid colour 0 (else they would turn transparent in an ILBM save). With Current the document keeps its colour ranges (they describe the palette that stays); with New it has none, as in ADR-9.
  - A picture whose colours are all in the current palette maps exactly without dither.
  - Settings are remembered at Ok (Cancel keeps the earlier ones); the colour count is remembered also while Current ghosts it.
  - Keyboard: Tab moves a focus over Position, Palette, Colours and Dither (skipping Colours while it is ghosted; the focused label is drawn in the select pen, nothing is focused at first) and the arrow keys change the focused option. The other requesters move Tab only between text fields; this one has none, so Tab moves between the option groups instead. Shift+Tab is not told apart from Tab (`ReqKey` has no Shift+Tab, as in every requester).
  - Layout and wording ("Import Picture", "Centre", "Top left", "Colours:", "-> WxH on WxH"), the one-pixel face gap between the thumbnail and its frame (so a black margin is told apart from the frame), the requester's size limits.
  - Anim ▸ Load.. of a PNG/JPEG keeps ADR-9 (the owner named Picture ▸ Load.. only).
  - After the review: the ordered dither's two-colour mixing (above); a picture's own colours win over the margin's (the margin takes the nearest picture colour when the picture uses every entry of the count; confirmed by the owner, see Owner decisions); the default count in whole bitplanes; only a new press of Return answers the requester; the focus highlight bar and whole-row ghosting; the toolbox marks under the requester; the cell grid for nearest colours; the 2-megapixel threshold for the background job and no "working" sign while it runs (the old thumbnail stays until the new one is made).

- Consequences:
  - The thumbnail is the result made small by area averaging, so a dither pattern mostly averages out in it (banding without dither and the palette's colours stay visible); the page after Ok shows the pattern.
  - A picture that uses every entry of the chosen count and does not contain the BG colour gets margins in the nearest picture colour, not the BG colour (e.g. a brown margin around a 32-colour sprite on a black BG); choosing a larger count, or Current, gives the BG colour.
  - On pages of 2 megapixels and more the thumbnail lags behind a click by up to about 1.5 s (release) without a sign that it is being made.
  - A smaller or larger picture no longer changes the screen in the GUI; to get a picture's own screen, the Screen Format requester comes first (or `--open`).
- Not verified:
  - By hand in the real window, with a real keyboard and mouse (the owner did not grant remote control).
  - Real photos and real PNG/JPEG files from other programs (only self-made pictures; ADR-9's notes on CMYK and progressive JPEGs still apply).
  - Windows and Linux (CI only builds and tests there).
