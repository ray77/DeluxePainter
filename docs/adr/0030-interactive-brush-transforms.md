# ADR-30: Interactive brush transforms, spare brush and Remap

- Status: accepted (Phase 3 follow-up, 2026-11-10; decided while the owner was away, following docs/research/manual-ui-notes.md section 2.2)
- Context:
  - ADR-19 left the Brush menu items that need a drag (Stretch, Any Angle, Shear, Bend, Place), the spare brush and Remap ghosted.
  - A brush change is not a document change: the brush lives in the app (`custom_brush`), the page and its undo history are untouched. Brush > Restore (`B`, the last picked or loaded brush) is the way back, as in DeluxePaint ("some manipulations are not reversible by it").
- Decision:
  - **Pure operations** in `dpx_core::brush_ops`, with unit tests: `stretch` (nearest neighbour, 1..=1024 per side, handle keeps its relative place), `rotate_any` (clockwise degrees, nearest neighbour, bounding box grows, new area transparent, handle turns with the pixels; multiples of 90 degrees give exactly the pixels of `rotate_90` and the flips), `shear` (top row fixed, bottom row moved by dx, rows in proportion), `bend` (parabola through both edges and the bend line; `displace` shared with shear), `handle_place`, `remap`, `fill_colour`. New area gets the brush's transparent colour, else the lowest palette colour no pixel uses, else 0.
  - **Gestures** (`tools.rs`, `ToolState.xform`): the menu item arms the gesture; the brush then follows the pointer (top-left at the pointer, for Place its handle) in the page preview. The press fixes the anchor, dragging previews the result (the brush in its own colours, Matte), the release replaces `custom_brush`. A click without a drag changes nothing (except Place). Any other action, a tool change or Esc drops an armed gesture. While armed, the normal hover brush and tool presses are suspended.
    - **Stretch**: the press is the top-left corner, the pointer the bottom-right (size = distance + 1). Shift keeps the aspect.
    - **Any Angle**: the press is the bottom-left corner; the bottom edge follows the line to the pointer (pointer to the right = 0 degrees, above = counterclockwise). Shift snaps to 15 degrees. A second use starts from the original orientation (the brush before the first rotation, as long as the current brush is still its result), as the notes describe.
    - **Shear**: the press is the top-left corner; horizontal pointer distance moves the bottom row.
    - **Bend Horiz / Vert**: the press is the top-left corner; the pointer's distance along the bend direction is the bend (rows move sideways for Horiz, columns up/down for Vert), its distance along the other axis sets the bend line.
    - **Place**: the brush is shown with its handle at the pointer; the press fixes the brush, dragging moves the handle marker (complementary colour), the release sets it.
  - **Spare brush**: `Brush->Spare` copies the custom brush into `ToolState.spare_brush`, `Brush<->Spare` swaps (nothing when either is missing). This is the hidden spare *brush* of the manual, unrelated to the spare page of Picture > Spare, so `MenuData.spare` is untouched. The spare brush is not saved. Metamorph stays ghosted.
  - **Remap**: a brush stores indices into the page palette and its colours are fitted to the page palette when it is loaded (ADR-19). The app therefore keeps `ToolState.brush_palette`, the page palette at pick-up or load. Remap maps every brush colour, as that palette had it, to the nearest colour of the current palette (transparent colour keeps its index) and takes the current palette as the new snapshot. Without a snapshot (brush made by other means) or with an unchanged palette it does nothing.
  - `--brush-xforms 'OP[:ARGS]@X,Y;...'` snapshot option (with `--pick`): stamps transformed copies of the picked brush (`stretch:W,H`, `rotate:DEG`, `shear:DX`, `bend_h:AMOUNT,LINE`, `bend_v:AMOUNT,LINE`), used for docs/design/screenshots/phase3-brush-transforms.png.
  - Menu ids made live: `brush.stretch`, `brush.rotate_any`, `brush.shear`, `brush.bend_horizontal`, `brush.bend_vertical`, `brush.handle_place`, `brush.to_spare`, `brush.swap_spare`, `brush.remap`.
- Deviations from DeluxePaint IV:
  - The gestures preview the finished brush stamped on the page rather than DeluxePaint's outline drag; the anchor corners follow the notes but the exact on-screen feedback is a guess.
  - Place keeps the handle inside the brush ("any offset" in the notes; DeluxePaint may allow outside).
  - Bend uses a quadratic through the edges and the bend line; the original's exact curve is unknown.
  - Remap works from a palette snapshot because our brushes carry no palette of their own; DeluxePaint remaps from the brush file's palette. `Brush<->Spare` stays ghosted while no custom brush exists (the generic Brush menu rule), although DeluxePaint could bring the spare back.
  - Stretch and rotation are not repeated for AnimBrush cels (AnimBrushes do not exist yet).
- Verified: unit tests of every operation (sizes, handles, transparency, exact quarter turns, parabola points, remap), app tests of each gesture (armed preview, drag preview does not touch the brush, one brush change on release, no page revision change, Restore, Shift aspect, second Any Angle from the original, Escape and tool change drop the gesture), spare brush and Remap tests, a headless snapshot; CI gate.
- Not verified: the gestures with a real mouse in the window (drag feel, cursor feedback), Be Square pixel aspect (the gestures use square pixels), very large stretches in the live preview.
